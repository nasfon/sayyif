## 2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to operate the App.

### 2.1 Staff and user accounts

When an administrator creates a staff account, we collect:

- Full name
- Email address
- Phone number (optional)
- Role (super administrator, shop administrator, or cashier) and assigned shop
- Account status, last sign-in time, and audit information (who created or last modified the account)

Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are stored only as **bcrypt password
hashes**. We never store or have access to your plain-text password.

### 2.2 Customer information

To manage sales and credit accounts, shop staff record:

- Customer full name
- Customer phone number
- Customer email address (optional)
- Customer address (optional)

### 2.3 Sales, credit, and expense records

Our records link transactions to people and shops, including:

- Sales: cashier (staff member), optional customer, items, amounts, payment method, date and time
- Credit payments: customer, amount, payment method, and the staff member who received it
- Manual credit adjustments: customer, amount, reason (free text), and the staff member who created it
- Expenses: description (free text), amount, date, and the staff member who recorded it
- Stock movements: the staff member who recorded each movement
- Audit logs: staff user, action, affected record, reason (free text), shop, and date and time

### 2.4 Business and receipt information

- Shop details: name, phone, email, address, logo, and receipt footer
- Business settings: business name, phone, address, logo, and receipt footer
- Receipts generated by the App: business and shop details, receipt number, date and time,
  customer name (or "Walk-in / Guest"), items, quantities, prices, totals, payment method, and
  any remaining credit

### 2.5 Authentication and session data

We use our authentication provider to sign you in with your email address and password. Session
tokens are stored on your device so you remain signed in, and are refreshed periodically.

### 2.6 Information we do **not** collect

The App does **not** collect:

- National identification numbers, government IDs, or biometric templates
- Date of birth, gender, or marital status
- Bank account, debit card, or credit card numbers
- Precise location data
- Contacts, calendar, photos, microphone, or camera data
- Advertising identifiers or device fingerprints

Payment methods are recorded only as a category (for example cash, card, transfer, or credit).
No card or account numbers are captured or stored by the App.

The Android app requests only the **INTERNET** permission, which is required to reach our servers.

## 5. How We Store and Protect Information

- Data is stored in the **Supabase** platform, which provides our authentication service and a
  PostgreSQL database accessed through a secured API.
- The App uses a public client key protected by **Row Level Security**, so each shop can only
  access its own records. Administrative keys are kept server-side and are never shipped in the App.
- Passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes. Sessions use signed tokens with refresh.
- We enforce role-based access control (super administrator, shop administrator, cashier) and
  validate input on both the device and the server.
- User, sale, credit, and expense events are recorded in audit logs.
- Data is transmitted over **HTTPS**.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. While we work to protect your
information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information. We share it only as follows:

- **Service providers (sub-processors):** We use **Supabase** for authentication, database, and
  backend functions, and our hosting provider to serve the App. These providers process data on
  our behalf.
- **Receipt and report sharing:** When a user chooses to print, save, or share a receipt or
  report, the App hands the file to the operating system's share sheet. The user may then send it
  through an app of their choosing, such as **WhatsApp** or **email**. This includes the
  customer's name and transaction details. Once shared with a third-party app, that app's own
  privacy policy applies.
- **Legal requirements:** We may disclose information where required by law or to protect our
  legal rights.

## 7. Data Retention

- Sales, sale items, credit payments, stock history, manual credits, and audit logs are retained
  as permanent financial and accountability records.
- Products, customers, and users are **soft-deleted**: when a record is deleted, it is marked as
  deleted and hidden from normal use, but the underlying data is retained in the database to
  preserve the integrity of historical transactions and audit records.
- We retain records for as long as needed to operate the business and to meet legal, tax, and

- Backups are taken regularly and retained for at least seven (7) days.
