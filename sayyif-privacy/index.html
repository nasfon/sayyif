1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how SAYYIF PRIMIUM MASTERS FLOUR NIG LTD (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Sayyif inventory, sales, customer-credit, and expense application (the “App”).

The App is a business tool provided to our staff and used to record shop operations, including sales and customer accounts. It is not a consumer sign-up service: accounts are created by our administrators, and there is no public self-registration.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information described in this policy.

Data controller: SAYYIF PRIMIUM MASTERS FLOUR NIG LTD (trading as “Sayyif”), Nigeria
Privacy contact: sayyifpremiumflourmasternigltd@gmail.com / 08064786344

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to operate the App.

2.1 Staff and user accounts

When an administrator creates a staff account, we collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Role (super administrator, shop administrator, or cashier) and assigned shop
  • Account status, last sign-in time, and audit information (who created or last modified the account)

Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are stored only as bcrypt password hashes. We never store or have access to your plain-text password.

2.2 Customer information

To manage sales and credit accounts, shop staff record:

  • Customer full name
  • Customer phone number
  • Customer email address (optional)
  • Customer address (optional)

2.3 Sales, credit, and expense records

Our records link transactions to people and shops, including:

  • Sales: cashier (staff member), optional customer, items, amounts, payment method, date and time
  • Credit payments: customer, amount, payment method, and the staff member who received it
  • Manual credit adjustments: customer, amount, reason (free text), and the staff member who created it
  • Expenses: description (free text), amount, date, and the staff member who recorded it
  • Stock movements: the staff member who recorded each movement
  • Audit logs: staff user, action, affected record, reason (free text), shop, and date and time

2.4 Business and receipt information

  • Shop details: name, phone, email, address, logo, and receipt footer
  • Business settings: business name, phone, address, logo, and receipt footer
  • Receipts generated by the App: business and shop details, receipt number, date and time, customer name (or “Walk-in / Guest”), items, quantities, prices, totals, payment method, and any remaining credit

2.5 Authentication and session data

We use our authentication provider to sign you in with your email address and password. Session tokens are stored on your device so you remain signed in, and are refreshed periodically.

2.6 Information we do not collect

The App does not collect:

  • National identification numbers, government IDs, or biometric templates
  • Date of birth, gender, or marital status
  • Bank account, debit card, or credit card numbers
  • Precise location data
  • Contacts, calendar, photos, microphone, or camera data
  • Advertising identifiers or device fingerprints

Payment methods are recorded only as a category (for example cash, card, transfer, or credit). No card or account numbers are captured or stored by the App.

The Android app requests only the INTERNET permission, which is required to reach our servers.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Create and administer staff accounts and control access by role
  • Record and process sales, customer credit, payments, expenses, and stock
  • Calculate customer balances, shop performance, and reports
  • Generate and share receipts and business reports
  • Maintain audit and accountability records for financial and inventory transactions
  • Secure the App, prevent misuse, and troubleshoot problems
  • Comply with legal, tax, and accounting obligations

We do not use personal information for advertising, profiling, or automated decision-making.

4. Legal Bases

Where data-protection law such as the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) or the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, we process personal data on the following bases:

  • Contract / legitimate business interest: to operate the App and record business transactions
  • Legal obligation: to keep accounting, tax, and audit records
  • Legitimate interests: to secure the App and prevent fraud or misuse
  • Consent: where required, for example before sharing a receipt through a third-party app

5. How We Store and Protect Information

  • Data is stored in the Supabase platform, which provides our authentication service and a PostgreSQL database accessed through a secured API.
  • The App uses a public client key protected by Row Level Security, so each shop can only access its own records. Administrative keys are kept server-side and are never shipped in the App.
  • Passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes. Sessions use signed tokens with refresh.
  • We enforce role-based access control (super administrator, shop administrator, cashier) and validate input on both the device and the server.
  • User, sale, credit, and expense events are recorded in audit logs.
  • Data is transmitted over HTTPS.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. While we work to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data location: Our organisation is based in Nigeria. Data is stored on the infrastructure of our service providers, which may process and store it in Nigeria or in other countries.

6. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information. We share it only as follows:

  • Service providers (sub-processors): We use Supabase for authentication, database, and backend functions, and our hosting provider to serve the App. These providers process data on our behalf.
  • Receipt and report sharing: When a user chooses to print, save, or share a receipt or report, the App hands the file to the operating system’s share sheet. The user may then send it through an app of their choosing, such as WhatsApp or email. This includes the customer’s name and transaction details. Once shared with a third-party app, that app’s own privacy policy applies.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information where required by law or to protect our legal rights.

7. Data Retention

  • Sales, sale items, credit payments, stock history, manual credits, and audit logs are retained as permanent financial and accountability records.
  • Products, customers, and users are soft-deleted: when a record is deleted, it is marked as deleted and hidden from normal use, but the underlying data is retained in the database to preserve the integrity of historical transactions and audit records.
  • We retain records for as long as needed to operate the business and to meet Nigerian legal, tax, and accounting requirements.
  • Backups are taken regularly and retained for at least seven (7) days.

You can ask us to correct inaccurate account or customer details at any time.
